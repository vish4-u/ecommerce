Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you come to our home or venue?

Yes. I travel for bridal and engagement bookings; charges depend on distance. You can also visit me in Kunda.

How early should I book a bridal date?

Two to four weeks ahead is best, and earlier in wedding season. Message me as soon as your date is fixed.

Will the makeup suit my skin tone and type?

Yes. I match foundation, concealer and blush to your undertone, and choose products for oily, dry or sensitive skin.

How long does it take?

Party makeup takes about 45 to 60 minutes. Bridal takes 2 to 3 hours including hair and draping.

Do I need to pay in advance?

A small advance holds your date. The balance is paid on the day of service.

Can I do a trial before the wedding?

Yes. Message me to book a trial so we can finalise your look.
